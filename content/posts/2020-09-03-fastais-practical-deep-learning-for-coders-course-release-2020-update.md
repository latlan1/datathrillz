On Aug 21st, 2020, fastai released a new version of their Practical Deep Learning for Coders -Part 1 course. This course is a must-take for new and intermediate deep learning practitioners. It is well done and teaches you intuition without drowning you in theory. The only prerequisites are some high-school math, and a year of coding experience (preferably in Python). This course is free and can be done without any installation, by taking advantage of the Colab and/or Gradient platforms, which provide free, GPU-powered Python notebooks. Go [here](<https://course.fast.ai/>) to learn more about the course.

## Fastai v2 is out!

Fastai has updated its foundation libraries to be faster and more flexible. [fastcore](<https://fastcore.fast.ai/>), [fastscript](<https://fastscript.fast.ai/>), and [fastgpu](<https://fastgpu.fast.ai/>) are the newly added foundational libraries used in fast.ai v2, and should be useful to many programmers and data scientists.

Fast.ai also released a new book called ‘[Deep Learning for Coders with fastai and PyTorch: AI Applications Without a PhD](<https://www.amazon.com/Deep-Learning-Coders-fastai-PyTorch/dp/1492045527>)’ which is available for purchase now and will be the basis of planned Part 2 of the Practical Deep Learning for Coders course. You access the Jupyter notebooks, which are the basis of the book [here](<https://github.com/fastai/fastbook>) for free.

Check out the [course ](<https://course.fast.ai/videos/?lesson=1>)and [release details](<https://www.fast.ai/2020/08/21/fastai2-launch/>)!
